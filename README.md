## 📖 About

**Germany UniHub** is a simple web platform created to help students explore **Master's programs in Computer Science and related fields at German universities**.

The goal is to make the university-search process easier by bringing relevant programs together in one place.

Instead of searching through many university websites individually, students can use Germany UniHub to quickly explore available programs and identify universities that match their interests.

### 🎯 Main Goals

* 🔎 Discover MSc Computer Science programs
* 🏫 Explore universities across Germany
* 📍 View universities by location
* 📚 Compare different study programs
* 🔗 Access official program information
* 🇩🇪 Make the Germany Master's application search easier

---

## 📋 Application Research

Before applying to a program, students should independently check:

* Bachelor's degree requirements
* Minimum CGPA / grade
* ECTS requirements
* English language requirements
* German language requirements
* GRE / TestAS requirements
* APS requirements, where applicable
* Uni-Assist requirements
* Application deadlines
* Tuition and semester fees
* Required documents
* Program-specific admission requirements

Germany UniHub is intended as a **discovery and comparison tool**, not a replacement for official university admission information.
