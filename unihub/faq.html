Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Germany UniHub and searching for university programmes in Germany.

1. What is Germany UniHub?

Germany UniHub is a free, student-focused platform that helps you discover and compare Bachelor's (BSc) and Master's (MSc) programmes offered by universities in Germany.

2. Is Germany UniHub free to use?

Yes. Germany UniHub is free to use. You can explore available programme information and use the platform to support your university search.

3. Which degree programmes are listed?

Germany UniHub aims to cover both Bachelor's and Master's programmes across different academic fields. The programmes available depend on the current dataset.

4. Where does the programme information come from?

Programme information is compiled with reference to the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) and other relevant sources where applicable.

5. Is all the information on Germany UniHub up to date?

We aim to make programme information useful and accessible, but requirements, deadlines, tuition fees, and application procedures can change. Always confirm the latest information on the official university website before applying.

6. How can I find a suitable programme?

Use the available search and filter options to narrow down programmes by degree level, subject, university, or other supported criteria. Review each programme's requirements carefully before shortlisting it.

7. Does Germany UniHub show admission requirements?

Where available, the platform provides information about admission criteria, language requirements, deadlines, and tuition fees. Details may vary by programme and university.

8. Are all universities in Germany included?

Not necessarily. The platform's coverage depends on the programmes and universities included in its dataset. The absence of a university or programme does not mean it is unavailable in Germany.

9. Does Germany UniHub guarantee admission?

No. Germany UniHub is an information and research tool. Admission decisions are made by the respective universities according to their own eligibility criteria and selection procedures.

10. Can I apply directly through Germany UniHub?

Germany UniHub helps you research programmes. Applications must be submitted through the official application portal or process specified by the relevant university, including uni-assist where applicable.

11. Does Germany UniHub provide visa or scholarship services?

Germany UniHub is primarily a university and programme discovery platform. It does not guarantee scholarships, admission, or a student visa. Please consult official university, scholarship-provider, and German visa information sources for relevant guidance.

12. What should I check before applying?

Before submitting an application, verify:

  • Academic eligibility and minimum grade requirements
  • Required documents and certified translations
  • English or German language proficiency requirements
  • Application deadlines and intake availability
  • Tuition fees and semester contributions
  • Application procedures and any additional tests

Always rely on the official programme page for final confirmation.

13. Who created Germany UniHub?

Germany UniHub was designed and developed by _rar, a senior software engineer and a Germany applicant himself. He built this platform to make finding the right university and programme easier for students, and to help people through his work — so the research journey feels clearer, lighter, and a little happier for everyone.

Important Notice

Germany UniHub is an independent educational information platform and is not an official DAAD website or a representative of any German university or government agency. Information is provided for general guidance only. Always verify official requirements and deadlines before applying.
