About Germany UniHub

Your Guide to Study Opportunities in Germany 🇩🇪

Germany UniHub is a free, student-focused platform designed to help students explore and compare Bachelor's (BSc) and Master's (MSc) programmes at universities across Germany.

Our goal is to make university research easier by bringing programme information into one convenient place, helping students make informed decisions about their academic future.

What You Can Do

  • Discover Programmes: Explore universities and degree programmes across different academic fields.
  • Search & Filter: Find programmes based on your preferred subject, degree level, and other available criteria.
  • Compare Requirements: Review admission criteria, language requirements, application deadlines, and tuition information where available.
  • Explore University Locations: Discover universities across Germany using location-based features.
  • Plan Your Applications: Organise your university search and shortlist programmes that match your goals.

Our Data Sources

Programme information is compiled with reference to the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) and other relevant sources where applicable. We aim to make academic information easier to access and compare.

Please note: Information may change between application cycles. Always verify admission requirements, deadlines, tuition fees, and application procedures on the official university website before applying.

Our Mission

We believe that finding the right study programme should be simpler and more accessible. Germany UniHub was created to reduce the time and effort required to research universities, allowing students to focus on preparing strong applications and planning their future.

Built with Care ❤️

Germany UniHub is an independent project by _rar, a senior software engineer and Germany applicant. He created this site to make university research easier — especially for students exploring opportunities in Germany from Bangladesh and beyond — and to help people through his work so their journey feels clearer and a little happier.
